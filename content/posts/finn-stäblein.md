Hey, I'm Finn.
 
I grew up in the scenic landscape of Southern Germany with a love for sports, science, and the outdoors. I started rowing when I was 10, and the sport shaped my life immensely for the next 8 years. It taught me the value of discipline, perseverance, and shaping a purpose. At the age of 18, I realized a dream by representing Germany on the national rowing team, winning gold at the Junior World Championships in the Men’s Double.
 
Driven by the same desire of creating a purpose, I developed an interest in entrepreneurship. So, at 17, I moved to Berlin to join Flink, Europe’s fastest-growing startup at the time.
 
Now at Stanford, I’m majoring in Computer Science with a focus on Biomedical Computation and am excited about the potential of AI to revolutionize this field. Last summer I was using Machine Learning at Juna.ai to improve the efficency of an oil reffinery and in doing so reduce the emissions needed to power our cars and heavvy industries.
